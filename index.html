Smart Energy Microgrid & Demand Forecasting Engine

Team: Bucephala | HackSphere 3.0

Catalyst Architecture & Pipeline

  • Forecasting Engine: Trained regression pipeline running via Zoho Catalyst QuickML.
  • Inference Service: Automated feature mapping (weather & telemetry) via predict.py.
  • Smart Switching Logic:
    • NORMAL_GRID_MODE: Standard baseline operation.
    • STORAGE_MODE: Peak hours (18:00–22:00) or high demand (>160 kW).
    • CRITICAL_ALERT: Overload risk (>=200 kW).
